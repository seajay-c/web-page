01 — Services

Two offers. Both for businesses.

A retained desk for the systems you already run, and a design practice for the route that gets a business connected. Not a home internet shop.

Managed IT

A desk with your name on the contract

Help desk, monitoring, endpoints, Microsoft 365, backup, and security. Sold as a retainer, with a named 15-minute commitment to first response.

  • One queue for incidents and requests
  • Monitoring that pages a person, not a mailbox
  • Backup checks you can ask about by name
How the desk works
Right-of-way

3D design and the permit to build it

Route and structure design in the public right-of-way, a permit package, and coordination with the roadway authority so a business lateral can actually be built.

  • Pole, conduit, cabinet, and clearance called out

  • Built for commercial sites, not residential plans

07 — Engagement

            
How an engagement starts.

          

          
Three things a buyer should be able to point at before signing. All of them are part of the retainer or the design scope, not an extra product.

        

        

          

            01
            
A named response

            
Fifteen minutes to first response on the retainer, around the clock. The clock starts when the ticket is opened, not when someone next checks mail.

          

          

            02
            
One incident, one record

            
Alert, diagnosis, the change, the check that failed and then passed, and the note the client actually received. Ticket 1842 above is the shape of that record.

          

          

            03
            
A permit you can track

            
Survey, 3D route, authority review, build window. The roadway office stays in the loop, and the business sees the same status we do.
