About us

We build networks that disappear into daily life — invented company story for this GitHub Pages demo.

Mission

Deliver reliable, affordable connectivity to every community on the island, with transparent pricing and human support in Greek and English.

Our story

Founded in 2012 as a regional ISP in Nicosia, Cyprus Communications expanded into fiber backhaul in 2016 and launched consumer 5G in 2022. Today our fictional footprint spans four districts with a NOC in Limassol and retail partners in every major town.

We invest in coastal resilience, underground duct sharing, and open APIs for municipal smart-city pilots — all narrative details for the showcase.
